Spotpreis Tibber

Fetches the hourly electricity prices from your own Tibber account, plus the consumption history and — with a Tibber Pulse — the instantaneous power second by second. Everything goes to Loxone: over MQTT and over a token protected HTTP endpoint.

First steps

  1. Get a token. At developer.tibber.com, sign in with your Tibber account and create one under Access Token.
  2. Settings: enter the token and save.
  3. Test tab, button Check account data. This is the most important click: it says in one line whether the token is accepted, what the home is called and whether a Pulse is connected.
  4. Test tab, button Fetch prices now — otherwise you wait for the next interval.
  5. MQTT: add the subscription in the gateway. Without it nothing arrives at the Miniserver.
  6. Loxone integration: create the import file, rebuild the blocks.

The difference to aWATTar and Octopus

Tibber delivers the final price ready made — including taxes and levies. With an exchange price you have to add grid fees, electricity tax, levies and VAT yourself; not here. That is why there is no price calculator, only a surcharge that defaults to 0.

The energy share and the tax share are still carried separately. That is not decoration: only the energy share can go negative, never the final price. To react to negative prices, evaluate NEG, not CUR.

Where they are the same quantities, the Loxone field names are the same as in the Spotpreis aWATTar and Octopus plugins. If you change supplier you swap the plugin — not the blocks.

Two price levels, and why

LEVEL is the level by your thresholds (cheap / normal / expensive). TLEVEL is what Tibber itself makes of it — Tibber compares against recent days, you compare against a fixed number. On a uniformly expensive day Tibber will still say „cheap“ at some point, your threshold will not. Both are useful, so both are provided.

The Tibber Pulse

The instantaneous values arrive over a persistent connection, not by polling. A separate service handles this; start it on the Settings tab. Without a Pulse or Watty these values do not exist — that is not a malfunction, and the Test tab says so explicitly instead of showing a cross.

A Pulse value older than two minutes is no longer output. An instantaneous power from three hours ago is not an instantaneous power.

Tariff comparison

The monthly average price is formed from cost divided by consumption, not as the mean of the hourly prices. That is the difference between „what would it have cost“ and „what did it cost“: whoever washes at night pays less than the daily mean, and that is exactly what the comparison should show.

The standing charge does not enter. Unit rates are compared; the standing charge only appears alongside in the monthly report.

What is not verified

This release has not been measured against any Tibber account. What is established are the calculations — they run against fixed test values in the self test — and the WebSocket reimplementation, whose framing was checked against an independent implementation of RFC 6455. What is not established is whether the token is accepted and whether the field names still match this release. The Check account data button answers that in one line.
